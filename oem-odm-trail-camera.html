  • Confirm the exact model and regional version in the quotation.
  • Evaluate a sample against your intended recording, power and connectivity requirements.
  • Approve the customization scope, artwork and acceptance criteria in writing.
  • Confirm MOQ, prices, payment terms and the lead-time starting point.
  • Request model-specific compliance documents for your destination market.
  • Agree inspection, shipment and after-sales arrangements with the team.

OEM / ODM Questions

What can I customize?

Discuss logo printing, packaging and firmware requirements with our team. Availability depends on the selected model and the scope of the project.

What is the MOQ for my project?

Send the model, quantity and customization requirements. We will confirm the applicable MOQ in your quotation; branding an existing model and developing new hardware require different assessments.

How long does an OEM or ODM order take?

Request a project-specific schedule covering sample preparation, approvals and production. Confirm the start date and any dependencies in the quotation.

How do I choose a cellular model for my market?

Specify the destination country, intended carrier and SIM requirements. Confirm the exact LTE version and test a sample on the intended network before a bulk order.

Which documents should I request?

Ask for model-specific technical specifications and the compliance documents applicable to your destination. For lithium-battery products, also confirm transport documentation with your freight provider.
