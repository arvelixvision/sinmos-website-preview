SHENZHEN SINMOS TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD is a professional trail camera manufacturer founded in 2015, headquartered in Shenzhen, China — the global center of electronics manufacturing.

We specialize in designing and manufacturing high-performance hunting and trail cameras for global distributors, brand owners, importers, and outdoor retailers. Our products combine cutting-edge imaging technology with rugged outdoor durability.

CE / RED, FCC and RoHS documentation is available for the models listed below. We offer full OEM & ODM services to help your brand stand out.

  • Professional trail camera manufacturer since 2015
  • Model-specific CE / RED, FCC & RoHS documentation
  • Full OEM & ODM — logo, packaging, firmware, private label
  • Serving distributors, importers & brand owners worldwide
  • Reliable delivery to Europe, USA & global markets
