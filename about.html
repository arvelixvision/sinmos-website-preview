Arvelix Vision is the export brand of SHENZHEN SINMOS TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD, a professional trail camera manufacturer founded in 2015, headquartered in Shenzhen, China — the global center of electronics manufacturing.

We specialize in designing and manufacturing high-performance hunting and trail cameras for global distributors, brand owners, importers, and outdoor retailers. Our products combine cutting-edge imaging technology with rugged outdoor durability.

All products are CE, FCC, and RoHS certified, meeting the strictest standards for the European and North American markets. We offer full OEM & ODM services to help your brand stand out.
