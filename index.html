About Us

Arvelix Vision

Arvelix Vision is the export brand of SHENZHEN SINMOS TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD, a professional trail camera manufacturer founded in 2015, headquartered in Shenzhen, China — the global center of electronics manufacturing.

We specialize in designing and manufacturing high-performance hunting cameras for global distributors, brand owners, importers, and outdoor retailers. All products are CE, FCC, and RoHS certified.

We offer full OEM & ODM services including logo printing, custom packaging, firmware customization, and private label manufacturing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from our B2B buyers.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+
Samples from 1 pc. Logo printing and stickers: MOQ 100 pcs. Custom packaging: MOQ 1,000 pcs. Firmware customization and new hardware development: MOQ and feasibility confirmed by quotation.
Do you support OEM and ODM services?+
Yes. We offer full OEM & ODM services including logo printing, custom packaging design, firmware customization, and private label manufacturing. Your brand, your specifications.
What certifications do your cameras have?+
All products are CE, FCC and RoHS certified, fully compliant for European and North American markets. Certificates are available for download on request.
What is your delivery time (lead time)?+
In-stock samples are generally ready in 2 days. Private-label production generally takes 15–20 days after sample approval. Hardware development timing depends on customer requirements and is confirmed for each project.
Can I test a sample before placing a bulk order?+
Absolutely. We welcome sample orders (MOQ 1 pc) to verify quality, features, and compatibility before committing to large quantities. Sample cost is deducted from your first bulk order.
How do you ensure product quality?+
Every camera undergoes 100% inspection — image sensor calibration, trigger speed test, waterproof seal check, and 24-hour aging test — before leaving our Shenzhen factory.
