Buyer Questions

Which trail cameras support remote cellular monitoring?

PR801LTE, PR903G, PR802LTE and H2G are listed as 4G LTE models. Confirm the regional version, LTE bands, carrier compatibility and SIM/data-plan requirements for your destination before ordering.

Does Wi-Fi mean I can view a camera from anywhere?

No. Wi-Fi models provide nearby app connectivity; Wi-Fi alone does not provide cellular remote access. For remote sites, shortlist a compatible 4G LTE model and confirm network coverage.

Which models include solar power?

PR903G, PR903DW, H1W and H2G list a solar panel in their power configuration. Runtime depends on sunlight, settings and usage; solar power does not guarantee uninterrupted operation.

Does H2G use AA batteries?

No. H2G uses a built-in 7800mAh battery and a solar panel. It does not use AA batteries.

What is the difference between 850nm and 940nm night vision?

850nm infrared may show a faint red glow. 940nm is generally used for less visible illumination. Actual night-image quality and range depend on the camera, scene and settings; evaluate samples for your application.

What is the minimum order for private labeling?

Logo printing and stickers start at 100 pieces. Custom packaging starts at 1,000 pieces. Firmware and new-hardware project quantities are quoted separately.

How long do samples and private-label orders take?

In-stock samples are generally ready in 2 days. Private-label production generally takes 15–20 days after sample approval. New-hardware development timing depends on the requirements.

Does the maximum video resolution tell me the sensor resolution?

No. The table lists the maximum video resolution published on each product page. Confirm frame rate, native sensor resolution, encoding and sample footage for your selected model before ordering.
